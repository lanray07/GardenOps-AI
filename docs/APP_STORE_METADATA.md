**Description**

```text
GardenOps AI helps you plan and manage a productive garden from a balcony, backyard, or allotment.

Create a garden profile, generate planting plans, track weekly tasks, estimate potential crop value, and preview plant scanner results. The app is designed for beginners who want a simple way to grow food, save money, and understand what their garden could produce.

Features:
- Garden onboarding by location, size, sunlight, and goal
- AI garden planner structure with mock-first planning
- Garden task tracker
- Crop value and profit estimates
- Plant scanner image flow
- Local demo mode with optional Firebase sync
```
